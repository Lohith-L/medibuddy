How MediBuddy uses your health data

MediBuddy reads fitness data from Health Connect only after you allow it, and only to show it to you and your care team in the app and on the MediBuddy website.

What we read

  • Steps for today and the last 7 days
  • Sleep sessions from last night and the last 7 days
  • Heart rate: the latest reading and today's average
  • Active and total calories burned today

If Health Connect has no step data, MediBuddy can count steps with your phone's built-in step sensor. This needs the Physical activity permission.

What we do with it

  • We save one daily summary per day to your MediBuddy account so the website shows the same numbers.
  • We never write data to Health Connect, never sell it and never use it for advertising.
  • We do not share it with anyone except people you give access to your MediBuddy account.

How to stop

Tap Disconnect on the Health Data page. MediBuddy removes its Health Connect access and stops syncing. You can also remove access any time in Health Connect settings, and ask us to delete saved summaries from your account.
